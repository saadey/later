Later. Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Techie Bravo

Later is a note app made by Techie Bravo. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Later on Android, iOS or the web. The short version: your ideas stay on your device, and we don't collect anything.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Your ideas are stored only on your device. Techie Bravo never receives them.
  • Later works fully offline and makes no network requests for its features.

What is stored on your device

To work, Later keeps the following on your device only:

  • The ideas you write, with their creation and edit times and whether they are active, done or archived.
  • Your settings (for example paper colour, haptics, sounds, the bin lock and the weekly reminder).
  • The text you are still writing, so a half-written note isn’t lost if the app closes.

What we collect

Nothing. Later does not send your ideas, settings, usage or any device identifiers to Techie Bravo or to anyone else. We have no server that receives data from the app.

Permissions on Android

  • Biometrics (fingerprint or face): only if you turn on “Lock the Ideas Bin”. The check is done by your phone’s operating system; Later only learns whether it succeeded and never receives fingerprint or face data.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the weekly reminder. It is a local notification created on your device.
  • Vibration: for the optional haptic feedback when a note is tossed.

Sharing and backups

If you share text into Later from another app, it is placed on a new note on your device. If you export a backup, Later creates a file and hands it to the destination you choose (for example Files, Drive or email); what happens to it there is governed by that service.

On Android, your device’s own backup feature (Google backup) may include app data, depending on your device settings. That backup is managed by Google and your account, not by us.

Deleting the app deletes all ideas stored in it.

The web version

The web version stores your ideas in your browser’s storage on your device. The website is a set of static files; like any website host, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) when the files are downloaded. Later itself sends nothing.

Links to other places

The About page links to Google Play (to see our other apps or rate Later) and lets you email us. Those open in other apps and are covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

Later is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date, and shown in the app.
